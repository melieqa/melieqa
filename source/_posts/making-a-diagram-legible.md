A diagram fails in a specific way: the reader understands every element and still cannot state the argument.
Everything is drawn, nothing is said.

## What usually goes wrong

- Too many claims in one image
- Hierarchy carried by colour alone
- Arrows that describe movement when the point is a relationship

## Where to start instead

Write the sentence the diagram has to prove. Draw only what the sentence needs. Everything else belongs in a
second image, or nowhere.
