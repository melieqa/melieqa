Developed analytical graphics for 12 architectural projects, helping design concepts become clearer for
presentations and client communication.<!-- more -->

The brief was rarely "draw this". It was closer to: the idea is good, nobody outside the studio can see it
yet. The work was finding the argument already inside each project and giving it a structure a reader can
follow in seconds.

## What a set usually contains

- A concept diagram that states the argument in one image
- Site and climate analysis, reduced to what actually drives the design
- Circulation and programme diagrams
- A sequence that carries a jury from the first glance to the plan
