I graduated with a cardiac hospital designed around the patient's path to critical care.<!-- more -->

Every plan decision was measured against one question: how many minutes, doors and decisions sit between
a person arriving in distress and the moment care begins.

Somewhere in defending it I realised I was more interested in how a design gets understood than in how it
gets detailed — which is more or less the job I do now.
