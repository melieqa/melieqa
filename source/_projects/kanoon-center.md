A children's library and workshop centre in a wind-scoured arid village, planned around Sistan's
120-day wind.<!-- more -->

Vernacular systems — a *Kharkhona* evaporative wall, a wind-powered *Asbad* pump — are reinterpreted as
child-operable devices, so passive cooling becomes something learned rather than hidden.

The programme is grounded in a thematic analysis of the spatial memories of 24 former Kanoon members:
what they remembered was rarely the architecture as drawn, and almost always the architecture as used.

## The argument

The building is not a container for a library. It is a working demonstration of how this climate has
always been survived, handed to the people least likely to be told how it works.

## Where it stands

Ongoing. Research and concept design are complete; the drawing set and final boards are in progress.
